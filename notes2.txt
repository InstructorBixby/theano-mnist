tutorial does sqrt(6/(in+out)) which for the first layer is 0.068, so my
numbers were about 7 times too small.

with weights that small everything going into tanh is near zero, and tanh
near zero is basically a straight line, so my hidden layer was doing
nothing that a plain linear model couldnt already do. thats why 500 units
bought me nothing.
